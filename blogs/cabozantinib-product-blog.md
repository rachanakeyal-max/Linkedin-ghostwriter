## Description

Cabozantinib is an oral **tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI)**. It blocks several proteins that tumours use to grow and to build new blood vessels, including **MET, VEGFR-2, AXL and RET**. Cutting off these signals slows tumour growth and spread.

It was developed by Exelixis and is sold under two brand names that **cannot be swapped for each other**:

- **Cabometyx® (tablets: 20 mg, 40 mg, 60 mg)** is approved by the US FDA for:
  - **Advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC)**, on its own or first-line with nivolumab
  - **Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC)** in patients previously treated with sorafenib
  - **Differentiated thyroid cancer (DTC)** that has progressed after VEGFR-targeted therapy and is radioactive-iodine refractory (patients aged 12 and older)
  - **Neuroendocrine tumours (pancreatic and extra-pancreatic NETs)** that were treated before (patients aged 12 and older; FDA approval March 2025)
- **Cometriq® (capsules: 20 mg, 80 mg)** is approved for **progressive, metastatic medullary thyroid cancer (MTC)**.

## Usage / Dosage

Cabozantinib must be prescribed and monitored by an oncologist. Typical label doses are:

| Product / Use | Usual daily dose |
|---|---|
| Cabometyx alone (RCC, HCC, DTC, NET) in adults | **60 mg once daily** |
| Cabometyx with nivolumab (first-line RCC) | **40 mg once daily** |
| Children 12+ (DTC, NET) weighing under 40 kg | **40 mg once daily** |
| Cometriq (medullary thyroid cancer) | **140 mg once daily** |
| Moderate liver impairment (Cabometyx) | **40 mg once daily** |

**How to take it:**
- **Take it on an empty stomach.** For Cabometyx, take it at least **1 hour before or 2 hours after** a meal. For Cometriq, do not eat for **2 hours before and 1 hour after** a dose.
- Swallow the tablet whole with water. **Do not crush, chew or open it.**
- **Missed dose:** if your next dose is due in **less than 12 hours**, skip the missed one. Never take a double dose.
- **Avoid grapefruit and grapefruit juice.** They can raise the amount of drug in your blood.
- Your doctor may lower the dose if you take strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (such as ketoconazole) or raise it if you take strong inducers (such as rifampin).

Treatment usually continues until the disease progresses or the side effects become too hard to manage.

## Side Effects

Most people get some side effects. Many can be managed with a dose pause, a lower dose or supportive care.

**Common side effects:**
- Diarrhoea, nausea, vomiting and constipation
- Tiredness and weakness
- Loss of appetite and weight loss
- **Hand-foot syndrome** (red, painful or blistering palms and soles)
- High blood pressure
- Mouth sores and taste changes
- Hoarse voice and hair colour changes
- Abnormal liver tests and low thyroid function

**Serious side effects (get medical help right away):**
- Severe bleeding, including vomiting or coughing up blood, or black stools
- Tears in the stomach or bowel (perforations) and fistulas
- Blood clots, stroke or heart attack
- Severe high blood pressure
- Jaw bone damage (osteonecrosis of the jaw)
- Protein in the urine and kidney problems
- Liver damage
- **RPLS**, a rare brain condition that causes headache, confusion, seizures and changes in vision

## Warnings / Precautions

Tell your doctor about all your health conditions and all your medicines before you start. Key precautions from the prescribing information:

- **Surgery:** stop cabozantinib at least **3 weeks before planned surgery**. Restart no sooner than **2 weeks after major surgery**, and only once the wound has healed.
- **Dental work:** stop at least **3 weeks before invasive dental procedures** to lower the risk of jaw bone damage. Keep up good oral hygiene.
- **Bleeding:** do not start cabozantinib if you have had a recent serious bleed.
- **Blood pressure:** your blood pressure must be under control before you start, and it will be checked regularly.
- **Monitoring:** expect regular liver, kidney (urine protein), thyroid and calcium tests.
- **Adrenal problems:** these have been reported when cabozantinib is used with nivolumab.
- **Drug interactions:** avoid St John's wort, grapefruit and strong CYP3A4 inhibitors or inducers unless your doctor approves.
- **Fertility and pregnancy:** cabozantinib can harm an unborn baby. Use effective contraception during treatment and for **4 months after the last dose**.
