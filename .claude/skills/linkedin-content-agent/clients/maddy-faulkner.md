## Comment style notes
- Default: fun, casual, Gen Z-leaning tone. Always lead with this unless told otherwise — don't default to the sharp/analytical voice for Maddy anymore.
- Sharp, insight-forward one-liners are a fallback only when explicitly asked for "insight" on structured/authority posts (business frameworks, leadership lists, career advice) — apply the post's idea to a fresh, concrete scenario rather than restating it.
- Casual/dry-humor voice (see `comment-voice-casual.md`) for posts that are themselves personal, confessional, or joke-driven (e.g. agency-life humor, self-deprecating personal stories, meme-style posts) — this overlaps heavily with the Gen Z default above.
- Vary keyword vocabulary across comments — don't repeat the same one or two terms every time.
- Follows the shared defaults in `comment-defaults.md` (positive tone by default, short, no lines lifted from the post, no plain summarizing).
