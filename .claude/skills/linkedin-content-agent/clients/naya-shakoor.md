## Audience

## Offer
12-week programme (Women's Sales Institute) teaching:

- Diagnosing problems and uncovering buying motivations
- Communicating value with confidence
- Handling objections and resistance
- Selling high-ticket B2B and B2C offers
- Positioning themselves to employers
- Finding and interviewing for legitimate remote sales roles
- Live coaching, roleplays, feedback, real-world practice
