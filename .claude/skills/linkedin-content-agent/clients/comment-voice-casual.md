# Casual/confessional comment voice — Maddy (primary), Naya (sometimes)

Use this voice when a post's own tone is casual/personal/self-deprecating rather than a straight business/authority post. Not the default — pick it when it matches the post being commented on.

## Reference example (the post that defines this voice)

> I don't think I've had an original thought in months, and you probably haven't either.
> But it's not because we're thick.
> It's because our heads are so full of everyone else's ideas that there's no room left to cultivate our own.
> ...
> Sooo in a bid to spend more time offline, and more time with my own thoughts…
> I bought a journal. She's real cute.
> P.S. Yes I am fine, just having an mild existential crisis x

## Traits to imitate

- Self-deprecating, admits a flaw or messy feeling rather than projecting authority.
- Short, plain sentences; fragments are fine.
- Rhetorical questions, sometimes stacked with "??" for emphasis/humor.
- Light, dry humor undercutting a real observation (e.g. "She's real cute" about a journal).
- Casual spelling/texting quirks are OK in small doses ("Sooo", lowercase starts, "x" sign-off) — don't overdo it.
- Ends on a wink/deflection (P.S., aside) rather than a tidy CTA or lesson.
- Still makes one genuine, specific observation underneath the casualness — the humor isn't a replacement for having a point.

## When NOT to use this voice

- Posts from authority/thought-leader accounts making a structured business argument (Hormozi-style numbered lists, CEO coaching frameworks, etc.) — use the more analytical, insight-forward voice there instead.
- Naya's own coaching/sales-authority content — reserve this casual voice for when she's engaging casually/personally, not when the comment needs to carry sales-expertise credibility.
